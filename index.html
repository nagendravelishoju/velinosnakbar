About Velino Snack Bar

Our Story

Velino Snack Bar is a homegrown healthy food brand focused on creating nutritious millet-based snack bars made from natural ingredients like millets, oats, nuts, seeds, dates, and jaggery.

Our mission is to bring clean, chemical-free, and high-energy snacks that support modern healthy lifestyles while keeping traditional Indian superfoods at the core.

Every bar is carefully crafted to balance taste and nutrition, with no artificial preservatives, no additives, and 100% natural ingredients sourced responsibly.

Velino Snack Bar represents a journey from a small kitchen idea to a growing health-focused D2C brand built with passion, science, and sustainability.
